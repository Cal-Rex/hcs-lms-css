National Occupational Standard

This qualification fulfils health care compliance and reflects all current legislation and standards for both Hospital and Company staff present in patient-facing areas.


The primary aim of this qualification is to safeguard patients and staff by risk managing access to patient-facing areas, improving infection control, ensuring that the right people have the right qualifications
and the standard of training necessary to be in a hospital setting.


This qualification is for life, it is a National Occupational Standard
and recognised by all credentialing schemes and registers.
